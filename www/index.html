Presets

Tap to run. ⋯ to rename or delete. Presets are saved on the Pi, so every phone sees the same ones. Save new ones from the Effects tab.

Kids mode

Once a PIN is set, any phone or tablet opens in Kids mode until someone enters the PIN on it. After that, that device stays in grown-up mode, even after closing the browser, until you tap 🔓 Log out. Changing the PIN logs out every other device. This is a kid lock, not real security: anyone with your FPP web password can still get in.
